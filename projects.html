Nutritio: Edge-First AI Nutrition Platform

A “ChatGPT for food”: a single conversational agent that lets users track meals, plan groceries, and get restaurant picks just by talking to it, instead of juggling separate apps. Built the agent's tool-calling architecture spanning 20+ tools across meal and macro tracking, pantry and receipt intake, recipe planning, and live restaurant search, all backed by persistent SQLite/Supabase state that gives the agent durable memory of a user's pantry, goals, and history. Fine-tuned a custom Gemma 3-1B architecture with regression heads for multi-target nutrient prediction, a 4x accuracy improvement over the zero-shot baseline. Not open source, but available soon!
